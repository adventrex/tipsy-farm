The Land

We farm what the soil survey
says we should.

Twenty-six acres in USDA zone 7b–8a: 174 frost-free days and an 80-to-110-day dry summer — a Mediterranean climate wearing an Oregon zip code. Instead of fighting that, each NRCS soil unit grows what its native analog grows.

61A
Foehlin gravelly loam · 4.1 ac

Orchard-rated ground — the old pear logic. Future home of the saffron rows: 300 corms go in this October.

108B
Manita loam · ~12 ac

The working backbone: cucumbers and row crops, pasture for the flock, cover-cropped rotations, and the pollinator hedgerows the hives commute along.

33A
Coker clay · Payne Creek

Riparian ground along the creek — buffer plantings, not production. Some acres earn their keep by simply being left alone.
